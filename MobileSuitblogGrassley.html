Grassley Defense Systems - Beguire Pente

Jeturk Heavy Machinery                                     Ochs Earth                                   Grassley Defense Systems                 Shin Sei Development Corporation

Designed specifically as an anti-Gund format mobile suit, the Beguire is equipped with a combat shield that disables Gundam weapon systems. While lacking any unique weapons this suit's greatest strength is being deployed in coordinated groups to disable and overwhelm a Gundam format mobile suit

Main Armaments:

Kinetic Shield - The mobile suit's signature weapon, this shield emits a pulse that interferes with Gundam weapon systems rendering them useless for an engagement such as the Aerial's swarm drones

Beam Rifle - A standard energy rifle used for mid to close range fights

Beam Saber - A standard beam blade to be used together with the suit's shield after disabling Gundam weapons
