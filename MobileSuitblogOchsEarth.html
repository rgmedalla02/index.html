Ochs Earth Corporation - Lfrith-Ur

Jeturk Heavy Machinery                                     Ochs Earth                                   Grassley Defense Systems                 Shin Sei Development Corporation

An older mobile suit design from Earth that still uses polluting physical ammunition long outlawed by the various colonies. These mobile suits are piloted by the infamous witch pilots that used forbidden gundam technology that directly interfaced with their body

Main Armaments:

Gattling Gun - A rotary cannon used by the Earth Mobile suits that fires kinetic rounds in violation of the ban against polluting physical ammunition in space

Phased Array Cannon - Heavy energy weapon mounted on the back of the suit used for wide sweeping attacks with its energy beam requiring heavy direction and only unlocked with a higher permet score of the pilot

Beam Saber - A close-range weapon that projects a beam blade to be used together with the suit's gun shield
