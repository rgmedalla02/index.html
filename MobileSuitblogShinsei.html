Shin Sei Development Corporation - Gundam Aerial

Jeturk Heavy Machinery                                     Ochs Earth                                   Grassley Defense Systems                 Shin Sei Development Corporation

Hailing from the planet Mercury, the Aerial suit incorporates swarm drones as part of its defense and offensive weapons. The signature suit of Suletta Mercury, she has an unsually close bond to it resembling that between siblings with her as the elder sister.

Main Armaments:

Beam Rifle - Standard issue primary arm present in all mobile-suits used for short to medium distance engagements

Beam Sword - Aerial's main melee weapon used for finishing blows in duels or engage in melee

Combat Shield - Aerial's unique personal shield that can deflect sword strikes or beam rifle shots, the shield can break off to form the remote swarm for offense or reform to protect Aerial from any threats

Swarm Drones - The drones that make up part of the Aerial's armor, these drones are armed with their own beams and can attack autonomously to confuse and overwhelm opponents
