Jeturk Corporation: Guel's Dilanza

Jeturk Heavy Machinery                                     Ochs Earth                                   Grassley Defense Systems                 Shin Sei Development Corporation

Guel's custom Dilanza mobile suit with its unique magenta color scheme and plume. The Jeturk' line of suits is known for their bulkier frames, beam weapons and flamboyant colors. It was destroyed after its first and last duel with Suletta's Aerial

Main Armaments:

Beam Rifle - Standard Issue side arm present in all mobile-suits used for medium distance engagements

Beam Partisan - Signature energy pole arm of Dilanza Units that resemble staves when not activated and extends the range of the suit when engaging in melee

Beam Torch - Side melee weapon used as a back-up to the Beam Partisan
